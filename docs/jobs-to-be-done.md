# Jobs To Be Done

Each job describes what the person needs to get done whether or not this app exists. None of them mention the app, a screen or a button.

---

## J-1 Bill Payer: get paid back in full

> "When I have paid a shared bill on my own, I want to know exactly how much each person owes me, so I can be paid back in full without chasing anyone for small missing amounts."

## J-2 Bill Payer: share the tip fairly

> "When the group agrees to leave a tip on top of the bill, I want the tip shared out along with the bill, so I am not quietly left covering the tip myself."

## J-3 Bill Payer: avoid sending wrong amounts

> "When I am working out what people owe in a hurry, I want to notice straight away if I have got a figure wrong, so I do not send people amounts I later have to correct."

## J-4 Group Member: trust the amount before paying

> "When someone asks me to pay them back for a shared bill, I want to see how my amount was reached, so I can pay promptly and be confident I am not being overcharged."
